Duo Stats skill tracker: setting it up (once per computer)

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc. It records the order you level your abilities during League games and sends it
to the Duo Stats app, so the Live tab can show skill orders for ARAM Mayhem. It only reads this computer's own game.

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (if you move or delete it, the extension stops working).
2. In your browser, open  chrome://extensions  (Edge: edge://extensions).
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the  duo-stats-skill-tracker  folder.
5. Pin the extension (puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar), then click it:
     - Duo Stats address: the address you open Duo Stats at, e.g.  http://kevins-macbook-pro.local:8765
       (on the Mac running Duo Stats itself:  http://localhost:8765)
     - PIN: the Duo Stats PIN (leave it empty on the Mac running Duo Stats)
     - Save, and allow the permission the browser asks for.
6. The first time you're in a game: click the extension, then "Open the game's data page". The browser warns that
   the connection isn't private (League uses its own certificate). Click Advanced, then Proceed. You can close the tab.

From then on it works by itself during games. Click the extension any time to see what it's recording.
The browser may show a "developer mode extensions" notice when it starts; that's normal for extensions loaded this way.
